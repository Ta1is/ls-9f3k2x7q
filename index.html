Fielding Rotation Rules

Wall — infield + outfield strength

Puts a Strong-rated player on each side of the field. Infield sides (SS+3B / 2B+1B) are checked no matter which outfield detail mode you're using; the outfield sides (LF+LC / RC+RF) only apply once Outfield detail is set to Specific.

Line — front-to-back pairs

Checks the spot directly behind each infielder: SS/LC, 3B/LF, 2B/RC, 1B/RF. Flags it when both players in a pair are rated Weak. Only applies with Outfield detail set to Specific.

Keep players apart — line

Separate from ratings — pick specific players who should never end up in one of those same front-to-back pairs together, no matter how they're rated.

Keep players apart — wall

Pick specific players who should never share a side of the field together (left or right, infield or outfield) — for any reason you choose, not just ratings.
